About PDF Compression

Our PDF compressor tool helps you reduce the file size of your PDF documents while maintaining acceptable quality. This is useful for email attachments, web uploads, and saving storage space.

The tool supports three compression levels:

  • Low Compression: Minimal quality loss, moderate size reduction
  • Medium Compression: Balanced approach for most use cases
  • High Compression: Maximum size reduction with noticeable quality loss

All processing happens directly in your browser - your files are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure when using PDFTools?
Yes, absolutely! All file processing happens directly in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.
Are there any file size limits?
Our tools can handle most standard PDF files. For very large files (over 100MB), processing may take longer depending on your device's capabilities.
Do I need to create an account to use PDFTools?
No, all our basic tools are available without registration. You can use them immediately without creating an account.
What browsers are supported?
Our tools work on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of your browser.
Are watermarks added to processed files?
No, we don't add any watermarks to your files. The output files are completely clean and ready to use.
