Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PDFMagic and PDF to image conversion.

What is PDFMagic?
PDFMagic is a free online tool that converts PDF documents to high-quality images in various formats including JPG, PNG, and WebP. It's designed to be fast, secure, and easy to use without requiring any registration.
Is PDFMagic free to use?
Yes, PDFMagic is completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or usage limits. You can convert as many PDFs as you need without any cost.
What file formats does PDFMagic support?
PDFMagic supports converting PDF files to JPG, PNG, and WebP image formats. Each format has its own advantages: JPG for photographs, PNG for documents with text, and WebP for modern web use with better compression.
How long does conversion take?
Conversion time depends on the size of your PDF and the number of pages. Most standard documents convert in under 30 seconds. Larger files with many pages may take a few minutes.
Can I convert specific pages instead of the whole document?
Yes, you can select specific pages or page ranges to convert. Use the "Pages to Convert" option to choose between all pages, specific ranges (like 1-5, 7, 9-12), or selected individual pages.
What's the maximum file size I can convert?
PDFMagic can handle PDF files up to 100MB in size. If your file is larger, we recommend splitting it into smaller parts or using compression tools before conversion.
What image quality settings should I use?
For documents with text, use PNG format with high quality settings. For photographs, JPG at 85-90% quality provides the best balance. WebP offers excellent compression while maintaining quality for web use.
Does PDFMagic preserve text quality?
Yes, PDFMagic uses advanced rendering techniques to preserve text clarity and sharpness. For best results with text documents, we recommend using PNG format with high quality settings.
What browsers are supported?
PDFMagic works with all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera. For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of your browser.
Is my data secure with PDFMagic?
Absolutely. PDFMagic processes your files entirely in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your computer and are not stored on our servers. Once you close the page, all data is automatically deleted.
Do you store my converted files?
No, we do not store any of your files. The conversion happens locally in your browser, and once you download your images, all temporary data is cleared. Your privacy is our top priority.
Can I use PDFMagic for sensitive documents?
Yes, because all processing happens locally in your browser, your sensitive documents never leave your computer. This makes PDFMagic safe for confidential business documents, personal files, and any other sensitive materials.
