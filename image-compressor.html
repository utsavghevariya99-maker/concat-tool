Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image?

Simply drag and drop your image onto the upload area or click "Select Image" to choose files from your device. Adjust the compression level and format settings, then click "Compress Image" to see the results.

What file formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF formats. You can also convert between these formats during compression.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes, the maximum file size is 10MB per image. For larger images, consider resizing them before compression or using our batch processing feature.

Are my images stored on your servers?

No, all image processing happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

What compression level should I use?

For web use: 70-80% compression. For social media: 60-70%. For maximum quality: 20-30%. For print: Use lossless compression or minimal compression.

How do I batch compress images?

You can select multiple images at once using the file selector (Ctrl+Click or Shift+Click). All selected images will be compressed with the same settings and can be downloaded as a ZIP file.

Why is WebP recommended?

WebP provides superior compression compared to JPG and PNG, with better quality preservation. It's supported by most modern browsers and reduces file sizes by 25-35% more than JPG.

How do I contact support?

You can reach our support team at support@compressify.com. We typically respond within 24 hours during business days. You can also use the Contact page to send us a message directly.

Troubleshooting Guide

  • Image won't upload: Check file size (max 10MB) and format (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF)
  • Compression seems slow: Large images take longer. Try resizing before compression
    • Download isn't working: Check your browser's download settings or try a different browser
  • Quality looks poor: Reduce compression level or try different output format
  • Tool not loading: Clear browser cache or update your browser

Quick Tips

  • Use WebP format for best compression results
  • Compress images before uploading to websites
  • Keep backups of original images
  • Use batch processing for multiple images
  • Adjust compression based on image content (photos vs graphics)
